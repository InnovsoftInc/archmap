## Architecture map

This project's architecture is documented in [`__DIR__/`](__DIR__/README.md): its services, data, outside services, the people who use it and the main flows, checked against the code.

- **View it:** run `__START__` and open http://127.0.0.1:4321/. Click a service to see its endpoints, jobs, libraries and environment variables as tables, and click a row for its details.
- **Check it:** run `__CLI__ check`.
- **Keep it current:** after a change that adds, removes or rewires something, ask your AI agent to "update the map". It runs `__CLI__ brief` and follows it.
